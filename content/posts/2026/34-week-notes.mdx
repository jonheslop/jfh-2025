Phew it’s been a busy 6 weeks and so I fell off the wagon posting wise.

## We moved house
We moved house and the build up to that was quite stressful. Even though we were in the process for six months our vendors were not ready and didn’t get their documents together until the 24 hours before completion so the exchange only happened the afternoon before we moved. Our buyers weren’t very happy about that. 

![Me waving bye to our old house](https://imagedelivery.net/tfgleCjJafHVtd2F4ngDnQ/IMG_5467/medium "caption")

The actual move day went pretty smoothly we had four removals guys who packed our stuff into two vans and we paid a bit extra for them to pack all the kitchen fragile stuff whilst we cleaned everywhere.

It was 34ºC on moving day which was a lot. We celebrated at the end by going to Hectors for a couple of wines and then back to Kate & Lianne’s where we’ll be staying for the foreseeable.

We’ve been working remotely from the house in the day which has been great for helping us get a sense of the place and think about what needs doing, then we cycle back into Hackney every evening.

So far we’ve done a lot of stripping out. All the carpets and laminate floors are gone. Ripped out the downstairs kitchen which was very gross and we’ve removed a lot of wallpaper.

We’ve had various calls with passive house consultants and a lot of chats with Ulf (our friend and architect) about how we’re gonna go about it all, it’s exciting but/and daunting.

The new house is going to be such a journey that I’ve set up a little website [retrophit.haus](https://retrophit.haus/) so I can blog about the process. It’ll probably be as haphazard as here but I’ll try my best, Bec might even write some posts too.

![Ad hoc living room in future bedroom](https://imagedelivery.net/tfgleCjJafHVtd2F4ngDnQ/L1010046-Web/medium "caption")

## Fforest Gather

We moved on a Wednesday and on the Sunday went to Fforest for a week for Gather. It was really great to take a beat and catch up with everyone. It was still very warm in Wales, I’ve never seen the fields so dry but the evenings were much cooler, making sleeping a lot more comfortable than in London. 

## Ithaca in 70mm IMAX

Last weekend I saw _The Odyssey_ at the BFI IMAX with Dov. We’ve been there to see all Nolan’s films since Inception I think. I enjoyed the experience it was so big, loud and overwhelming (complimentary). But the relentless plot moving and some dialog was tiring. I sympathise with a lot of the criticism it got but overall I still liked it. It did look amazing. Funny how Nolan got a dog recently and then made Argos the dog a central plot device or whatever.

## 5k eclipse special

On the evening of the eclipse I did a 5k with a few people from work as part of a slightly cringe tech event in Battersea Park. I’d never actually ran 5k before I started training a few weeks before, but was a good challenge. We did it in 25mins the others could have gone faster I think but was nice to run as a unit. As we finished we watched the eclipse from the river bank, we didn’t have any glasses so didn’t really get a good view but was fun all the same.

## Fox intruder 

Also on Saturday I left our back door open and a fox came in (possibly two different foxes) and did a gross wet poo on Bec’s Apple Magic Keyboard. Awful. The room absolutely stunk it had sprayed on a few things too, both of our back packs. I spent a good few hours sorting it all out. After thinking I could clean the keyboard, I removed some keys and then gave up and just ordered a new one. So horrible. I won’t be making that mistake again.
