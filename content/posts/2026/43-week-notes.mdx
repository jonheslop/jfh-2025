Autumn’s really here, had some crisp cold mornings here in Walthamstow early in the week. Nice light as the sun comes up over the marshes.

Then storm Austen and Boelo came in and that was a bit much. On Thursday evening I went to Wapping to meet Jim, Tom and Billy, and the ride home was hella wet, but I went prepared in my new North Face smock I picked up in second hand in Japan and thighs aside, I kept dry.

House wise we’ve been adding things in just to make things more confortable, there’s lots of terrible spot lights here with very cold white bulbs, so been putting in warmer LED bulbs and some proper under cabinet lighting in the kitchen. I put up some shelves in the kitchen for all our spices and bits and little free standing unit in the bathroom. It’s nice to get things out of boxes and be able to live more normally.

We’ve got two eps of _The Pitt_ to go and looking forward to seeing how they wrap it up. We got through _Slow Horses_ pretty quick, this series felt a little procedural but it was still funny and like gripping enough to keep it interesting, also not overly long, was surprised it was only 6eps. They were filming the next series on the high street here not long ago so look forward to trying to notice that.

On the weekend my parents came to visit Saturday and bought a dishwasher they’d procured from a friend who no longer needed it so that’s a very nice convenience to have.

On Sunday we went to meet Bec’s parents in Cookham with her siblings and had a pub lunch. On the way we got a flat tyre, we spent 40mins on the phone to The AA but gave up and just called someone local. We weren’t in a rush as made it to the pub but a joke that we pay a subscription only for them not to even answer the phone... there was a big screw in the tyre, first time I’ve actually got a flat. Quite mad modern cars dont have a spare tyre really.

I’ve got quite addicted to playing [(not) solitare](https://notsolitaire.com/) by the folks at [Puzzmo](https://www.puzzmo.com). It’s just normal solitare but you can see other playing, I’d describe it as cozy.
