Still mentally recovering from the fox incident [last week](/posts/2026/34-week-notes/). I feel nasally scarred.

## My brother’s wedding

On Wednesday we flew to Berlin for my brother’s wedding. Haven’t been there for a couple of years and was nice to be back. We stayed at the Michelberger Hotel on Warschauer Straße. It’s a little hectic around there but if you wander over the bridge into Kreuzberg it gets more chill. 

The rest of my fam were all in Mitte, we used Lime bikes to get over there a couple times, riding home down Karl Marx Alle is quite the trip (architecturally and historically speaking) but also a slog four days in a row. 

Anyway we managed to fit some good dinners, including at [Stoke](https://www.stokeberlin.com/), a yakitori place, which was deffo a highlight. Really [fancy custom speakers](https://www.handhifi.com/) too!

The wedding itself was really special, the venue was incredible, [Reethaus](https://flussbad.com/) which was right on the Spree quite far east in Köpenicker. 

It was this amazing shuttered concrete pavilion wrapped around a huge thatched tower which was the central room where the ceremony happened and then later became the dance floor. The food was also incredible and also completely vegan.

## Treptower Park Biergarten

Sunday was a slow day to put it mildly, but we did drag ourselves out for a walk around Treptower Park which I’d not been to before and it was really very nice, right on the River Spree, it was a lovely warm sunny afternoon which helped. We ended up at a huge beer garden, which was very busy. The vibe was great and it made me sad that we don’t have things like that in the UK really.

We also saw a man walking a long with a pigeon on his head... and the pigeon was wearing a nappy! We looked it up and this is indeed a thing (more commonly used for parrots), at first I thought he must have been making the nappies himself. So that was remarkable!

## The Pitt

We recently watched the first series of _The Pitt_ I really enjoyed and was properly sucked in. I quite like medical gore for one plus it’s good how it squeezes in all the societal issues. I did find the first 10mins of the first episode quite difficult the dialogue seemed so cliché and wooden, I was like WTF is this... but then I sorta settled in and mostly didn’t mind.
