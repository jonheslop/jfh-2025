Ok wow, seven weeks have gone by quick. Last time I wrote we’d just moved house, gone to Wales, then Berlin, worked whilst getting the house in order and then Japan for three weeks. We’ve been back two weeks. So lots to recap.

## The house

As of this Thursday we’re full time in the house. We have heating, a hot shower, an oven/hob. It’s quite shabby (chic) there’s no carpets, wallpaper, barely any plug sockets but we’re making it work.

We got planning permission to recombine to two halves into one house, so this weekend we opened up the dividing wall, it feels extremely luxe to be able to get into the living room without having to go outside and all the way around to the backdoor so that’s nice.

## Japan

I think I should do a whole post for Japan, it was great I loved it. We did/saw a lot, probably crammed too much in but there was so much to see. Our itinerary was:

- Tokyo
- Osaka
- Kōya
- Kyoto
- Kanazawa
- Takayama
- Matsumoto
- Toyama
- Noto
- Tokyo

Everything feels sorta chaotic but also extremely orderly and it seems like public services work _so_ well. Food and drink was reasonably priced and I loved the balance/juxtaposition between the old and the new... I particularly enjoyed the listening bars and I wish we had things like them here, and don’t say Spiritland because that almost feels like a entirely different thing. I think they couldn’t work here because of maybe planning and cost of living reasons.
