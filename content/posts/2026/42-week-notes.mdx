Ok wow, seven weeks have gone by quick. Last time I wrote we’d bought a new house, gone to Wales, then Berlin, worked whilst getting the house in order and then Japan for three weeks. We’ve been back two weeks. So lots to recap.

## The house

As of this Thursday we’re full time in the house. We have heating (upstairs), a hot shower, an oven and a hob. It’s quite shabby (chic) there’s no carpets, wallpaper, barely any plug sockets but we’re making it work.

We got planning permission to recombine to two halves into one house, so this weekend we opened up the dividing wall, it feels extremely luxe to be able to get into the living room without having to go outside and all the way around to the backdoor so that’s nice.

Our old place was all built in storage but Bec’s found us some great Habitat wardrobes and drawers on Facebook market place. We picked them up this weekend. I put up a shower screen so we no longer have to sit down shower and a load of shelves around the place.

I put up the Vitsœ shelves in the living room, it’s nice to have my home working setup back, albeit a little paired back.

In our old house we had a Mikrotik router attached to an old-ish Unifi access point, the new house is longer and has too many brick walls for the Wi-Fi to reach everywhere and so I bought another access point off eBay, but for it to seamless mesh I’ve dropped the Mikrotik for a Unifi Cloud Gateway Ultra and it’s so fancy! I couldn’t believe how easy it was to setup, I plugged it in and it basically sorted itself out. The admin panel is so fancy. Anyway I don’t really know what I am doing and it sorta seems ridic we have a setup made for a 300+ person office but it’s fun. It seems pretty easy to pick up cheap stuff on eBay as business are always upgrading.

We live about a mile from the dump but to get there in the car is a much longer round trip because of [LTNs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Low_traffic_neighbourhood) (complementary). So I’ve been gettin progressly better a packing large things onto my cargo bike which is way more fun anyway. Yesterday my bike with wood and cardboard on was at least 2m wide and 4m long... still nowhere near as large as a range rover but getting there.

## Japan

I think I should do a whole post for Japan, it was great I loved it. We did/saw a lot, probably crammed too much in but there was so much to see. Our itinerary was: Tokyo, Osaka, Kōya, Kyoto, Kanazawa, Takayama, Matsumoto, Toyama, Noto and back to Tokyo.

Everything feels sorta chaotic but also extremely orderly and it seems like public services work _so_ well. Food and drink was reasonably priced and I loved the balance/juxtaposition between the old and the new... I particularly enjoyed the listening bars and I wish we had things like them here, and don’t say Spiritland because that almost feels like a entirely different thing. I think they couldn’t work here because of maybe planning and cost of living reasons.

I daydreamed of being able to click my finger and swap every car in the UK for a [Kei car](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kei_car), they’re so good, think of all the space we’d all have.
